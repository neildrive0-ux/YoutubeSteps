name: Quote request privacy
description: Owner-confirmed sharing and retention rules for snow-removal quote requests.
---

The business does not sell or share quote-request information for other parties' independent purposes. Necessary service providers may process information to operate the website, store requests, and deliver email.

Quote-request records in the app database are to be retained for one calendar month, then automatically deleted. This does not delete copies held in email or third-party provider systems.

Ai Landscaping & Contracting Services Inc. is the legal business name and operates under the trade name Ai Landscaping & SnowRemoval. The business uses Meta Business Suite and Meta Pixel, plus two-factor authentication with no other business safeguards currently reported. Replit hosts the website and PostgreSQL database; Replit/Resend processing regions are unknown. The owner does not want a mailing address listed.

**Why:** The owner confirmed the legal entity and trade name, Meta Business Suite and Pixel use, two-factor authentication, one-month retention period, Replit as the database host, and no mailing address in the public policy.

**How to apply:** Keep the public privacy policy aligned with the database cleanup and state separately that external email/provider copies may follow their own retention practices. Disclose Meta Pixel's transmission of website activity to Meta separately from quote-request handling; do not describe sharing as absolute. Do not infer Replit/Resend processing locations or whether every account holding customer information has two-factor authentication enabled.
