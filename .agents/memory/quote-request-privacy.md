The business does not sell or share quote-request information for other parties' independent purposes. Necessary service providers may process information to operate the website, store requests, and deliver email.

Quote-request records in the app database are to be retained for one calendar month, then automatically deleted. This does not delete copies held in email or third-party provider systems.

Ai Landscaping & Contracting Services Inc. is the legal business name and operates under the trade name Ai Landscaping & SnowRemoval. The business uses Meta Business Suite and two-factor authentication, with no other business safeguards currently reported. Replit hosts the website and PostgreSQL database; the processing region is not confirmed.

**Why:** The owner confirmed the legal entity and trade name, Meta Business Suite use, two-factor authentication, one-month retention period, Replit as the database host, and distinction between independent sharing and necessary service-provider processing.

**How to apply:** Keep the public privacy policy aligned with the database cleanup and state separately that external email/provider copies may follow their own retention practices. Do not infer the Replit/Resend processing locations, Meta Pixel use, or whether every account holding customer information has two-factor authentication enabled.
