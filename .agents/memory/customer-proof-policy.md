---
name: Customer proof policy
description: Guidance for presenting testimonials and customer proof on the Ai Landscaping & SnowRemoval site.
---

Do not publish customer testimonials as factual endorsements unless the user supplies or verifies the quotes. Until then, present the same trust-building ideas as clearly labeled service standards rather than attributed reviews.

**Why:** Placeholder testimonial copy can look like a real endorsement after a visual redesign, which undermines trust and risks misrepresenting customer feedback.

**How to apply:** When verified reviews are provided, replace the service-standards section with the exact approved quotes and attribution details. Do not invent names, ratings, or customer statements.
